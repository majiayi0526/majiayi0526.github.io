Research focus研究方向

01

Computer vision on the piste剑道上的计算机视觉

Markerless motion capture and YOLOv8-Pose skeleton extraction turned into a 19-indicator biomechanics system for sabre technique.以无标记动作捕捉与 YOLOv8-Pose 骨架提取为基础，构建佩剑技术的 19 项生物力学指标体系。

  • YOLOv8-Pose
  • OpenCV
  • Markerless Mocap

02

Aligning models with expert coaches让模型对齐专家教练

Two-stage SFT + DPO alignment (Qwen2.5-7B + LoRA) so a model reasons along the diagnostic pathway a senior coach actually follows.两阶段 SFT + DPO 对齐（Qwen2.5-7B + LoRA），让模型沿着资深教练真实的诊断路径进行推理。

  • Qwen2.5-7B
  • LoRA
  • SFT + DPO

03

Injury risk, flagged early损伤风险的前置识别

Interpretable lower-limb injury-risk reports for sabre fencers aged 12–18, validated against national-level coach evaluation.为 12–18 岁佩剑运动员生成可解释的下肢损伤风险报告，并以国家级教练员评估作为验证基准。

  • Weighted Kappa / ICC
  • Expert Panel
  • Streamlit
