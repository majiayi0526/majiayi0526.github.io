On the sporting side, I am a National Class-I fencer, a Grade-A fencing referee and head sabre coach at the High School Affiliated to Peking University. Years on the piste and in the training hall have given me three vantage points at once — athlete, referee, coach — and an eye for turning a movement into a judgement others can follow.运动经历上，作为击剑一级运动员、A级击剑裁判以及北大附中佩剑主教练，长期的赛场和教学经验使我拥有了运动员、裁判、教练三重视角，擅长把动作讲成任何人都听得懂的判断。

On the academic side, my undergraduate and graduate years at BSU, together with a short exchange at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and a visiting year at Springfield College, drew me into research, in particular into making sports video structured and explainable.学习经历上，北体的本硕学习、香港中文大学的短期交流与美国春田学院的访学经历塑造了我对于科研的兴趣，开始学习体育视频的结构化与可解释化。

Working across computer vision, deep learning and large-model fine-tuning, and building full-stack systems myself with AI coding assistants (Python / OpenCV / ffmpeg; a React 19 + Vite + TypeScript front end; deployed on Vercel and in mainland China), I have delivered:我通过计算机视觉、深度学习与大模型微调，以及借助 AI 编码代理完成的全栈独立开发（Python/OpenCV/ffmpeg，React 19 + Vite + TypeScript 前端，Vercel 与国内部署），完成了一些成果：

  1. A fencing officiating-assistance system I developed, which aligns skeletal time series with broadcast footage frame by frame and links each call to the relevant FIE rule. It has been evaluated on disputed calls from the Paris 2024 Olympic Games, and I am working with the Haidian District Fencing Association to integrate it into the association’s official referee-training platform.个人开发击剑判罚辅助系统，把骨骼时序与转播画面逐帧对齐，并把每次判罚关联到对应的 FIE 规则条文，在巴黎 2024 奥运会争议判罚上完成评测，并与海淀区击剑协会合作参与其官方裁判培训平台搭建、接入该系统。
  2. My master’s thesis, which uses computer vision and a fine-tuned large language model to assess injury risk in youth sabre fencers.硕士论文用计算机视觉 + 微调大模型评估青少年佩剑运动员损伤风险。
  3. A pickleball teaching system for the PE Department of Beijing Information Science and Technology University. Using MediaPipe and DTW, it compares serves, forehands, backhands, volleys and other strokes against 8 reference templates, and returns a score, technical-parameter deviations, corrective feedback and a report.为北京信息科技大学体育部开发匹克球教学系统，通过 MediaPipe、DTW 比对发球、正反手、截击等 8 项标准动作模板，输出评分、技术参数偏差和纠错反馈、报告。
  4. Deep Court, the AI broadcast system my team built at the 12-hour BroadcastCode 12 hackathon (NBA × AWS × CSDN), with me as captain; it won the AI runner-up award. It takes a game clip and the NBA’s official advanced data, and confirms each player’s identity by combining YOLO detection and tracking, OSNet re-identification and jersey-number recognition with the official roster. An agent running on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore calls tools on its own to write the commentary and the on-screen plan; after narration is voiced with Amazon Polly, the output is a game video overlaid with player names, official metrics, tactical graphics and Chinese commentary.作为队长参加「深度赛场」BroadcastCode 12 黑客松（NBA × AWS × CSDN，12 小时），获 AI 亚军。系统输入比赛视频与 NBA 官方深度数据，通过 YOLO 检测跟踪、OSNet 重识别与球衣号码识别，结合官方名单确认球员身份；运行在 Amazon Bedrock AgentCore 上的 Agent 自主调用工具生成解说与画面计划，经 Amazon Polly 配音后，输出叠加球员姓名、官方指标、战术图示和中文解说的比赛视频。
